How to read an alert

A signal for investigation, not a root-cause verdict.

●
ALERT

Observed yield is below the comparison baseline by more than the configured threshold.

●
NORMAL

No threshold breach was detected against available comparison data.

●
INSUFFICIENT DATA

There are not enough eligible comparison lots to establish a baseline.
